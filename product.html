Sequoia Pro is the headphone we'd hand a producer mixing their first album. Every part — the magnesium frame, the bio-leather ear cups, the 40mm beryllium drivers — is built around a single goal: get out of the way of the music.

An on-device DSP tracks the acoustic profile of your room and adapts in real time. The result is a sound that stays even-tempered whether you're on a noisy train or a quiet studio.
